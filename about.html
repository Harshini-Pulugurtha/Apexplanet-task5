About SweetBite

Welcome to SweetBite Bakery & Dessert Shop — where every bite tells a story of sweetness and love! We bake daily using the freshest ingredients, bringing you the most delightful cakes, cookies, muffins, and desserts in town.

Founded in 2020, SweetBite started as a small home bakery and has now grown into a full-fledged dessert paradise. Our mission is simple — to make life sweeter, one dessert at a time!

  • Friendly service and cozy atmosphere
  • Free delivery on orders above ₹999
